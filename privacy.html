Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the LumiLoo video-production application (“the App”) handles data when it accesses Google APIs. The App is operated privately by the owner of the LumiLoo YouTube channel and is not offered to other users.

What the App does

The App generates short videos and uploads them to the operator’s own YouTube channel. After an upload it reads back the resulting video’s status (for example its privacy setting and processing state) to confirm the upload succeeded. It performs no other action on the YouTube account.

Google user data the App accesses

The App uses Google OAuth with the minimum scopes required:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload videos to the operator’s own channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read back the operator’s own channel and video details in order to verify each upload.

The App accesses only the operator’s own YouTube account. It does not access, collect, or process personal data belonging to any other person, and it does not read viewer, subscriber, comment, or analytics data.

How data is used and stored

  • The OAuth token issued by Google is stored solely on the operator’s own server, with file permissions restricting it to the operator, and is used only to call the YouTube Data API for the purposes above.
  • Video files and their metadata (title, description, tags) are created by the operator’s pipeline and sent to YouTube; they are not shared with any other third party.
  • No advertising, profiling, or analytics use is made of Google user data.

Data sharing

The App does not sell or transfer Google user data to third parties. Data is exchanged only between the operator’s server and Google’s APIs.

Limited Use

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and revocation

The OAuth token is retained only while the pipeline is in use and is deleted when no longer needed. Authorization can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access, which immediately ends the App’s access.
